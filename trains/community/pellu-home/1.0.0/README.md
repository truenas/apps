# Pellu Home Server

[Pellu Home Server](https://pellu.co/home-server/linux) shares the videos (and their subtitles) in the folders
you choose with the Pellu apps on your phones, tablets and TV at home.
Files play as they are: nothing is transcoded, and nothing is uploaded.

**!!! WARNING !!!**

This application requires additional elevated permissions beyond standard containerization protections. Use at your own risk.

The following additional privileges may be enabled from installing this application:

• Host Networking

• mDNS Announcements
