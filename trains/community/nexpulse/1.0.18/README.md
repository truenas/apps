# nexpulse

[nexpulse](https://github.com/DerKezorm/nexpulse) is a speed test tracker for the homelab. It measures
your internet connection on a schedule, shows every test live on a gauge, keeps the history with
charts and averages, and sends alerts over ntfy, Gotify or a webhook when the line is slower than
your plan.
