# nexcanvas

[nexcanvas](https://www.nexcanvas.de) is a whiteboard for your own server, in the spirit of
Apple Freeform: sticky notes, shapes, text, lines, pen and highlighter, photos and PDFs on an endless
board, edited live by several people at once. Frames and scenes, a shape library, templates, export as
PNG and PDF, and JSON Canvas to exchange boards with other apps.
