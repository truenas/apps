# nexcrate

[nexcrate](https://github.com/DerKezorm/nexcrate) manages movies, shows and music in one
self-hosted app, in place of Radarr, Sonarr and Lidarr. A movie or a show can have several
versions, such as Full HD and 4K, each with its own profile and folder. It searches Newznab and
Torznab indexers, downloads through the usual Usenet and torrent clients, and can take over the
library of an existing Radarr, Sonarr and Lidarr.
