# DNSalsa Connector

[DNSalsa Connector](https://github.com/DNSalsa/dnsalsa-docker) puts a service in your network online at your own [dnsalsa.nl](https://dnsalsa.nl) address through a Cloudflare Tunnel, without port forwarding. It pairs with your DNSalsa account by a short code; the route is set in the DNSalsa dashboard.
