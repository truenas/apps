# HexOS

**Everything you love, on a server you own.** HexOS puts your data and your apps on hardware under your roof, on your terms. As easy to use as a phone. No IT experience required.

**Already on TrueNAS? Get more out of it.** HexOS is a force multiplier for TrueNAS, not a replacement ([everything HexOS does](https://hexos.com/features)). Install it here and the Command Deck opens on top of the system you already have. Your pools, settings, users, shares and apps all stay put, just the way you have them. The full TrueNAS UI stays put too, so you always have the underlying controls when you want them. NFS, iSCSI, S3 and directory services keep running as they do today.

**Find an app you want. HexOS takes care of the rest.** Over a hundred apps, updated daily: photos, media, documents, passwords, a smart home, and VMs for Windows, Linux and more. Pick one, tap install, and it's ready the first time you open it. No YAML, no volume mappings. Apps are backed up nightly, so you can update with confidence. [Browse the app catalog](https://hexos.com/apps).

**Buddy Backup.** Back up to a friend's HexOS server, encrypted before it leaves, so only you can read it. They host it. You own it. Restore a folder, or a whole server. [Buddy Backup on hexos.com](https://hexos.com/features#backups).

**Everything on your server. Anywhere you are.** HexOS Anywhere gives each app its own https address through HexOS's relay, on with a switch. No port forwarding, no exposed IP, nothing for a visitor to install. Your network stays closed. [How HexOS Anywhere works](https://hexos.com/anywhere).

**Folders and shares, simplified.** Permissions you can read at a glance. Bring Over pulls your files in from a cloud account, another NAS or external drives. [Folders on hexos.com](https://hexos.com/features#folders).

**Health, monitored.** The Hardware Advisor matches your exact parts and hardware revisions against issues checked by real people, so you hear about them before problems arise. Drive Certification and Deep Check test a drive before you trust it with your data. Diagnostics run on your schedule, with a recommended fix when something is found. [Health on hexos.com](https://hexos.com/features#health).

**Notifications a human can read.** If a drive fails or a backup is missed, you'll hear about it in plain words, on the deck and by email for anything critical.

**Getting started.** Once the app runs, open its Web UI and choose **Claim this server**. Sign in at deck.hexos.com and enter this server's TrueNAS admin password. The deck opens read-only until the server has a HexOS license; get one at hexos.com.

**Already running HexOS?** Then there is nothing to install: this server is already on your deck. Installed anyway, this app does nothing and removes itself.
