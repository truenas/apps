# nextime

[nextime](https://github.com/DerKezorm/nextime) is a calendar for a family or a small group on your own server:
own calendars shared with members and guests, iCloud, Google and other CalDAV accounts read and written, and a
calendar account for iPhones and Android phones over CalDAV.
