# GeoPulse

[GeoPulse](https://github.com/tess1o/GeoPulse) transforms raw GPS data from OwnTracks, Overland, Dawarich,
GPSLogger, Home Assistant, Traccar, Colota and other sources into a searchable timeline of stays, trips, and movement
patterns. It runs fully on your own infrastructure and integrates with Immich, Memos, and Weather so photos, notes, and
conditions appear directly on your map history.
