# nextasks

[nextasks](https://github.com/DerKezorm/nextasks) keeps the tasks of a family or a small group on your own
server: spaces with members and lists, due dates and repeats, tasks assigned to several people, reports by mail and
notifications to the browser and the iPhone app.
