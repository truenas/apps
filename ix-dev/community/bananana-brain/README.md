# bananana brain

[bananana.dev](https://bananana.dev) is your own control room for servers, Macs and PCs. The brain keeps your machines'
live readings, alerts, automations, files, terminals and history on your TrueNAS system, and you reach it from anywhere
at your-name.bananana.dev and in the bananana.dev apps for iPhone and Android. It connects out through an end-to-end
encrypted relay, so it publishes no ports and needs no VPN.

To set it up, create a free account at [bananana.dev/start](https://bananana.dev/start), copy the setup token it shows
(it starts with **bst_**) into **Setup Token**, and install. The token is only used on the first start. Then open your
brain at the address bananana.dev gives you and add your other machines from its Nodes page.

The brain's data (identity, settings, users and history) is in the **Data** storage, so keep it backed up. A nightly
backup goes to /data/backups, or to /backups if you add a storage mounted there.

Help: [bananana.dev/help](https://bananana.dev/help). Support: [bananana.dev/support](https://bananana.dev/support).
