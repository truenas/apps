# nexpaper

[nexpaper](https://github.com/DerKezorm/nexpaper) is Paperless-ngx for the home, the simple version: a filing cabinet
for the papers of a household or a small firm on your own server, with an inbox, text recognition and search, vaults
per person and an import from Paperless-ngx.
